## Full description
**A cozy repair shop tycoon and management sim: diagnose broken things, fix them, and win a street's trust, one week at a time.**

**Run your own repair shop.** You inherit a one-bench repair café on Hollow Lane in a throwaway age. Every kettle, lamp, radio, bike, games console, phone and sewing machine that comes through the door is a small vote against the landfill.

**Diagnose, don't guess.** Customers describe the symptom. You choose the tests, read the results, and name the fault. A wrong call wastes the parts and the hours. 8 trades and 79 faults, each one a short deduction puzzle.

**Quote against the alternative.** Every customer knows what a new one costs and how much they care about this one. Price fairly, promise a date you can keep, and the street remembers.

**Grow bench by bench.** Three suppliers: cheap and slow, next week, or today at a premium. Four kinds of staff who learn on the job, careful fixes or quick ones, new benches and new trades. Five streets, each with its own reputation. Thirteen story customers whose lives thread through three years.

**Then decide what you became.** The beloved shop on Hollow Lane, FixEmpire, or the shutters coming down. Unless the shop goes bust, keep it open in endless mode.

**Your first year on Hollow Lane is free,** with two streets and the start of every story. One purchase unlocks years two and three, all five streets, the endings and endless mode.

- Turn-based: a week at a time, as long or short as you like. No timers.
- Offline game: no ads, no account, nothing else to buy
- Portrait play with large-text and high-contrast options
