Mended: Repair Shop

Run your own repair shop. Diagnose, quote, order parts and win a street's trust.

Get it on Google Play

Mended: Repair Shop is played in the Android app.

Run your own repair shop. You inherit a one-bench repair café on Hollow Lane in a throwaway age. Every kettle, radio, bike, console, phone and sewing machine that comes through the door is a small vote against the landfill.

Diagnose, don't guess. Customers describe the symptom. You choose the tests, read the results, and name the fault. A wrong call wastes the parts and the hours. 8 trades, 79 faults, each one a short deduction.

Quote against the alternative. Every customer knows what a new one costs and how much they care about this one. Price fairly, promise honestly, and the street remembers.

Grow bench by bench. Three suppliers with different lead times. Four kinds of staff who learn on the job. Five street blocks, each with its own reputation. Thirteen story customers whose lives thread through three years.

Then decide what you became. The beloved shop on Hollow Lane, or FixEmpire. Then keep going in endless mode.

  • No ads. No timers. No accounts. No consumables. Fully offline.
  • Turn-based: a week at a time, as long or short as you like.
  • Portrait, one-handed, large-text and high-contrast options.
  • Your first year on Hollow Lane is free, with two streets and the start of every story. One purchase unlocks years two and three, all five streets, the endings and endless mode.
